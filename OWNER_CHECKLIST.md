# Speechless owner decision and launch checklist

## First founder review

- [ ] Confirm the brand direction: modern, joyful, word-forward autism awareness apparel.
- [ ] Approve adult T-shirts as the first product category and decide when children’s sizing should follow.
- [ ] Review the four core designs and seven retained drafts: approve, revise, or retire.
- [ ] Approve the three audiences: For Me, Family & Caregivers, and Community & Allies.
- [ ] Calculate costs and approve pricing before any dollar amount appears publicly.

## Tommy’s story and community language

- [ ] Approve every public detail about Tommy, including use of his name and any future photography.
- [ ] Decide whether Amber wants to appear by name, in photographs, or in a first-person founder letter.
- [ ] Ask nonverbal people, autistic adults, caregivers, and families to review product language where possible.
- [ ] Confirm “nonverbal” as the brand’s chosen wording while respecting each person’s own language preference.
- [ ] Keep disclosure optional: the wearer’s dignity, comfort, consent, and privacy come first.

## Product development

- [ ] Choose and order adult unisex shirt blanks for comparison.
- [ ] Test garment fit, fabric weight, wash performance, shrinkage, and print quality.
- [ ] Finalize each graphic for front/back placement, ink count, print size, and garment colors.
- [ ] Confirm XS–4XL availability and obtain real garment measurements.
- [ ] Calculate garment, printing, packaging, labor, transaction, and shipping costs before setting final prices.
- [ ] Assign SKUs, variants, inventory quantities, and approved sellable status.
- [ ] Photograph real samples and replace all concept imagery before public sales.

## Legal and brand

- [ ] Complete business-name, domain, social-handle, and trademark clearance for “Speechless.”
- [ ] Confirm the legal business entity, address, support email, and operating jurisdiction.
- [ ] Have qualified counsel review privacy, terms, accessibility, returns, consent, and commerce policies.
- [ ] Confirm licenses or ownership for every final logo, typeface, graphic, photo, and written asset.

## Commerce and operations

- [ ] Choose Shopify or another reviewed commerce provider.
- [ ] Connect provider-hosted payment and checkout; never collect card numbers directly in this app.
- [ ] Define shipping regions, rates, processing time, taxes, exchanges, returns, refunds, and customer support.
- [ ] Test inventory, variant selection, discounting, checkout, confirmation, fulfillment, and refund flows.
- [ ] Choose packaging and include any brand card, care information, or community message.

## Marketing and privacy

- [ ] Select an email provider and verify the sending domain.
- [ ] Approve opt-in, unsubscribe, retention, and deletion practices before sending marketing email.
- [ ] Approve launch photography, social templates, founder announcement, and product copy.
- [ ] Decide whether launch proceeds with a small founding drop, preorder, or ready inventory.

## Public-launch gate

- [ ] Complete accessibility review with keyboard, screen reader, zoom, contrast, and reduced-motion checks.
- [ ] Test the site on representative phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.
- [ ] Review every page, form, link, metadata value, social preview, and empty/error state.
- [ ] Connect a durable production database with backups.
- [ ] Enable indexing only after brand, products, policies, inventory, and commerce are approved.

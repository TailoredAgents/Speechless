# Speechless logo exploration

**Selected direction:** Signal Cut. Production-ready outlined exports now live in `public/brand/`, and the compact signal mark is used by `src/app/icon.svg`.

The original three-direction study is retained here as the creative record behind the selected identity.

## 01 — Signal Cut (recommended)

A decisive apparel-style wordmark with a custom `H` whose coral crossbar becomes a signal path. The name remains intact rather than visually emphasizing “less,” while the yellow dot carries forward the brand's existing signal motif. It is the clearest, most ownable direction and scales best across the storefront, garment labels, packaging, and social avatars.

## 02 — Open Channel

A compact stacked system built around a blue communication line. It expresses that communication continues across forms and gives the identity a more editorial, movement-oriented voice. It is distinctive but occupies more vertical space in the website header.

## 03 — Human Signal

A softer fashion-editorial pairing of a bold sans serif with a warm italic serif. It feels personal and expressive, but the contrast is less legible at very small sizes and may date faster than Signal Cut.

## Design boundaries

- Fashion label first; mission-led without looking clinical or charitable.
- No puzzle pieces, crossed-out mouths, diagnostic shorthand, or claims about a wearer's identity.
- The wordmark must remain readable in one color and at small header size.
- A compact mark must work for social avatars, favicons, neck labels, and small embroidery.
- Production artwork uses outlined letterforms, so it does not depend on a local font installation.
